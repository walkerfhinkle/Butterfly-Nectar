Common allergens

Based on the ingredient list above, this product contains or is derived from:

  • Tree nut & seed oils: sweet almond oil, shea butter, coconut oil, sunflower seed oil, castor seed oil, rapeseed oil
  • Soy: soybean oil (Glycine Soja)
  • Essential oils & scent: amber essential oil, tea tree oil, and rosemary oil. Essential oils are a common cause of contact sensitivity in skin care
  • Preservatives: benzyl alcohol, phenoxyethanol, BHT
  • Botanical extracts: rosemary, sage, nettle, yarrow (Achillea Millefolium, related to ragweed and chamomile), horsetail, reishi mushroom, blackberry, turmeric

If you have a known allergy or sensitivity to tree nuts, coconut, soy, essential oils, or any botanical listed above, please review the full ingredient list carefully, or speak with Imani directly, before using this product.

Patch test first

Before applying any new skin product broadly, we recommend a simple patch test:

  • Apply a small amount of product (about the size of your fingertip) to a discreet area of skin, such as your inner arm, wrist, or leg.
  • Wait 48 hours without washing or disturbing the area, and watch for any signs of sensitivity.
  • Our products are thoughtfully crafted with natural ingredients. However, every skin type is unique and may react differently.
  • If redness, irritation, itching, or scaling occurs, discontinue use immediately. If symptoms persist, please consult a physician.

How to use & store

When you're ready to use and save for later:

  • Apply a small amount to clean, dry skin and massage in until absorbed.
  • For very dry areas (elbows, heels, hands), a slightly thicker layer can be left on overnight.
  • Store in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and heat.
  • Keep the lid closed when not in use to protect freshness.

When to check with a doctor

Speak with a doctor or dermatologist before use if you:

  • Are pregnant or nursing.
  • Have a diagnosed skin condition (eczema, psoriasis, dermatitis, or similar).
  • Have a known allergy to tree nuts, coconut, soy, essential oils, or any ingredient listed above. Reactions can be more frequent or more intense for known allergies than for general skin sensitivity.

This product is scented and intended for adult use; young adults may also use it. Do not use on infants or young children.

Discontinue use immediately if you notice redness, burning, itching, or irritation, and rinse the area with water. Seek medical attention if a reaction is severe or does not resolve.
